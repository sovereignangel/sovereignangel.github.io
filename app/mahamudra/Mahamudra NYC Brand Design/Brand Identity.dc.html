06 · Voice

Plain, unhurried, specific. Say what happens and when it happens. No promises about transformation, no exclamation marks, no borrowed mysticism.

Sanskrit terms keep their diacritics — Mahāmudrā — and are glossed once, in plain English, the first time they appear.
